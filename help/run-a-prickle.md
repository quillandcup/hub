# Run a prickle

This is how a regular prickle runs. Sprints and other flavors vary, but they start from here. For the room itself, see [how to join a prickle](/help/join-a-prickle).

## Before it starts

Join the room a minute or two early, in case Zoom needs to update. The previous prickle may still be wrapping up, so say hello or wait until they finish.

## Welcome everyone

Greet each person by name as they arrive, especially anyone new. Your main job, besides keeping time, is making everyone feel welcome and building relationships. The same faces often come back, and those relationships are what keeps writers accountable.

## Go around the table

Ask everyone what they're working on. If you remember something about their book or their characters from last time, say so. Aim to finish by minute 18, so everyone can start writing. This is the hardest part of hosting: some writers need extra time, and some are chatty, so redirect kindly.

## Start writing

Remind everyone: cameras on, microphones off. For trial or new members, a sentence on why helps (seeing others write makes us write too). Tell them you'll speak up at the top of the hour, so anyone who needs to go can, and anyone in flow can stay.

Some hosts ask at the start whether people want to be brought back at the top of the hour. Otherwise it's "write until you drop": ask people to share their progress in the chat before they quietly leave.

## End on time

Keep an eye on the clock, so nobody is late for what's next. About 5 minutes before the end, get everyone's attention and go around again to ask how the writing went. It gives everyone a moment to reflect, celebrate their progress and get inspired by the others.

## If you can't host

Post in `#prickle-times` as soon as you know. Ask if anyone can cover for you, and tag someone who regularly joins you. Don't wait until the last minute.
