# Start a pop-up prickle (PUP)

Anyone can host a pop-up prickle. If you want to write at a time when no prickle is scheduled, a PUP holds you to it and may bring company.

## Open the room

Select **Off air** (or **On air**) at the top of any page. When the room is empty, the button reads **Start a PUP on Zoom**. Open it, and whoever joins next sees the prickle light up.

![The room, off air, with Start a PUP on Zoom](img:join-a-prickle/02-on-air-popover.png)

## Invite others

Post your time in Slack in `#prickle-times`, so others can join you. For the best turnout, give one to two hours' notice ("anyone want to write at 7pm tonight?"). At least 10 minutes' notice is a good minimum.

You can also open the room to put yourself in a writing mindset, like sitting down at the kitchen table. In that case you don't need to announce anything, though fewer people will find you.
