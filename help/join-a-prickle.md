# How to join a prickle

A prickle is a scheduled writing session on Zoom. Find one that suits you, then use the link in the Hub to get in.

## See what's coming up

Your dashboard lists the next prickles, with the day, time and host.

![Your dashboard with the upcoming prickles](img:join-a-prickle/01-dashboard.png)

## Find one that works for you

Open **My Prickles**, then **Find a Prickle**. Choose when you can write (today, this week or the next two weeks) and a time of day, and the Hub shows the prickles that fit.

![Find a Prickle, asking when works for you](img:join-a-prickle/03-find-a-prickle.png)

## Get into the room

Every prickle uses the same Zoom room. Select **On air** (or **Off air**) at the top of any page to open it:

- When people are writing, the button reads **Join on Zoom** and the list shows who is there.
- When nobody is, the button reads **Start a PUP on Zoom**. A PUP is a pop-up prickle: open the room and whoever joins next sees it light up.

The meeting ID and passcode are listed under the button, in case Zoom asks for them.

![The On air list with the Zoom button, meeting ID and passcode](img:join-a-prickle/02-on-air-popover.png)
