# Become a prickle host

Hosts keep the prickles running. You welcome everyone, get the writing started and wrap up at the end. Other members plan around your slot, so hosting is a regular commitment.

## When you can host

We invite hedgies to host once they've been a member for a full calendar month. Before then, the **Hosting** tab under **My Prickles** asks you to settle in first. Join some prickles, get a feel for how they run, and come back.

![The Hosting tab for a newer member](img:become-a-host/01-settle-in-first.png)

## What you'll see once you can

The **Hosting** tab shows your hosting stats (prickles hosted, show-up rate, on-time rate and your most recent one), and a form to request a slot for the coming month.

![The Hosting tab for a host](img:become-a-host/02-hosting-tab.png)

## Request a slot

1. Select **Request to host**.
2. Choose **How often will you host?**: every week, every other week, once a month, or just once.
3. Click the day and time you'll host in the weekly calendar. Dashed blocks are other hedgies' proposed or confirmed slots, and times are shown in Eastern.
4. Choose the type of prickle: a regular prickle, a sprint, or another flavor you'd like to try.
5. Add a note if it helps, then select **Submit request**.

![Requesting to host every week](img:become-a-host/03-request-form.png)

Pick a time you can keep for at least the next month, because other members count on you to show up.

Your request is saved as proposed. An admin confirms it, and a confirmed slot joins the schedule for that month. The current month is locked, so changes you make apply to next month.

Once you're hosting, [how to run a prickle](/help/run-a-prickle) covers what to do in the room.
