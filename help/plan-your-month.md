# Plan your month of prickles

Writers who show up on a schedule write more. Pick a few regular prickle times, commit to them, and the Hub keeps count of the ones you made.

## Start from your goals

Think about what you want to finish this month, then how many prickles that takes. Some writers pick a rhythm and keep it (Monday, Wednesday and Friday at the same time). Others choose a few core times and drop into more when a free hour appears. Both work.

Rest is part of the plan. If you need to skip a prickle, skip it, and try not to miss two in a row.

## See the weekly schedule

Open **My Prickles**, then **All Prickles**. This is the recurring weekly schedule, also known as Prickle Times, with the day, time, type and host of each prickle, shown in your time zone.

![All Prickles, the weekly schedule](img:plan-your-month/01-all-prickles.png)

## Commit to the times you choose

1. Select **Make a commitment**.
2. Tick the prickles you'll show up to, in the table or the calendar. Pick as many weekly times as you like.
3. Choose **For** how many weeks, from 1 to 12. The Hub shows how many sessions that adds up to and the dates it covers.
4. Select **Commit to these**.

![Two prickles picked, for four weeks](img:plan-your-month/02-pick-and-commit.png)

## Track what you've kept

**Commitments** lists your active commitments. Each one shows how many sessions you've kept and how many are still to go, one dot per week.

![Active commitments](img:plan-your-month/03-commitments.png)

## Put your prickles on your calendar

At the top of **Commitments**, choose **Google Calendar**, **Apple Calendar**, **Outlook** or **Copy link**, and confirm when your calendar asks to add or subscribe. It's a one-time setup: the prickles you host and the ones you've committed to show up on their own and stay up to date, with a reminder 15 minutes before each.

Google can take 12 to 24 hours to pick up changes, and Apple and Outlook are usually faster. The link is private, so don't share it.
