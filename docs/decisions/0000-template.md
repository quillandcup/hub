# NNNN. Short title of the decision

Status: proposed
Date: YYYY-MM-DD

Copy this file to `NNNN-short-title.md` (the next free number, lowercase words joined by hyphens) and
fill it in. Delete this paragraph. Status is one of `proposed`, `accepted`, `superseded by NNNN`,
`rejected`. See `README.md` in this folder.

## Context

What forces the decision: the problem, the constraints, what was tried or considered. Plain facts, enough
for someone with no context.

## Decision

What we decided, in the active voice ("We will ..."). Include the options turned down and why, if that
helps the next reader.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we now have to do or live with.
