# Local Layer Tables

> **Status:** living. **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-16. Review: yearly. Kept up to date with the code; if it is wrong, fix it.

Operational data this app owns; never reprocessed (these tables are the source of truth). The layer rules are in `.claude/rules/data-architecture.md`. The first bullet lists the tables, the second the Hub-made events tables; the text is the catalog as it stood before it moved out of the rule file.

- `member_hiatus_history`, `member_name_aliases`, `ignored_zoom_names`, `restricted_slack_channels` (private Slack channels whose messages staff can't read; a row = restricted, every change audited and shown in the Activity Log's Privacy view; restricting clears the text already in `member_activities` and rebuilds leave it out; managed at `/admin/data/slack-channels`), `chat_channels`, `chat_channel_members`, `chat_messages`, `chat_message_contents`, `chat_reactions` (the read-only Hub mirror of bridged Slack conversations, projected from Bronze by `/api/process/chat`; ids are stable across reprocessing; content in its own table so RLS can gate it; service role writes; see `docs/product/SLACK_BRIDGED_CHAT.md`), `prickle_types`, `staff`, `calendar_feed_tokens` (secret per-member token for the subscribable `/api/calendar/feed/<token>.ics` feed), `calendar_feed_items` (prickles, weekly slots and events a member added to that feed by hand), `member_ask_me_about` (profile "Ask me about…" topics), `member_notes` (a member's private notes to self about another member; author-only RLS with no admin access, hidden in sudo), `prickle_checkins` (a member's check-in for a prickle: mood before and after on two labeled 1-5 scales, optionally the feeling named on the Feeling Wheel (Willcox, 1982), their intention, and whether they got what they came for; see `docs/product/CHECKINS.md`; readable by the member and admins, writable only by the member, read-only in sudo; option keys in `lib/prickle-checkins.ts`), `prickle_checkin_dismissals` and `prickle_checkout_dismissals` (prickles whose check-in offer or check-out a member dismissed: hidden from the dashboard, the search palette and upcoming prickles, and no Slack DM; the check-out one holds the dashboard's "What did you write?" dismissals), `notification_preferences` (a member's per-kind, per-channel overrides of the notification defaults in `lib/notifications/registry.ts`; no row = default; member-writable, admin-readable, read-only in sudo), `in_app_notifications` (what the in-app channel sent a member, kept for good: the `/notifications` inbox table, the latest few under the header bell, and a banner while unread and still time-sensitive (`OutboundMessage.timeSensitiveUntil`, `banner_until`); read when opened, its banner dismissed, "Mark all as read", or the feature resolves it with `resolveInAppNotifications`; server-written, member and admin readable, the member can only set `read_at`; hidden in sudo), `member_reminder_log` (reminders sent: intro in #start-here, prickle reminders; backoff and dedup for `/api/internal/member-reminders`, see `lib/member-reminders.ts`; service role only), `member_onboarding` (a member's Getting started tour: steps marked done by hand, dismissed/completed; other steps count as done from the member's own data, see `lib/onboarding.ts`; hidden and read-only in sudo), `push_subscriptions` (browsers a member turned Browser notifications on for: Web Push endpoint and keys, so service role only; `deleted_at` when turned off or the push service says it's gone; see `lib/channels/web-push.ts`), `media_assets` (the media library catalog, `media_` ids; `kind` is `image` today, videos and files later: an uploaded image, the original kept untouched in the private `media` bucket, with title, alt text, tags, focal point, owner and `is_public`; sized copies are derived on request by `lib/media/` and served at `/api/media/<id>/<preset>`; staff manage the shared library, a member their own; through `MediaRepository`; see `docs/product/MEDIA_LIBRARY.md`), `mentorships` (the Big Sister program: every new member, however they arrived, matched with a big sister for 30 days; `started_on` and `kind` come from the sign-up in `lib/arrivals.ts`, never typed; `outcome_note` is the big sister's reason a trial didn't continue, written through `set_mentorship_note`; admin only, since it names new members; whether a trial converted is derived from membership history in `lib/mentorship.ts`, never stored; the Big Sister badge counts them through `mentorship_counts()`, members read their own side through `my_mentorships()`), `big_sister_volunteers` (members who volunteer to be a big sister, soft-deleted with `deleted_at`; self-serve under the hosting eligibility rule; see `docs/product/BIG_SISTERS.md`), `outreach_prospects` (Instagram accounts that might be ideal Hedgies, with a rules-based fit score and a review status; admin only; `ProspectRepository`; see `docs/product/PROSPECTING.md`), `slack_identities` (Slack user id → Hub account allowed to sign in from Slack; server-written only) and `slack_sign_in_tokens` (hashed single-use button tokens and codes the Slack app hands out); see `lib/slack-sign-in.ts`, both service role only
- `hub_sessions` (the schedule of an event made in the Hub, which has no Google Calendar item: title, description, host, guest speaker, location, start and end; written through `HubSessionRepository`, whose `create`/`update` project it into a prickle (source `hub`, `prickles.hub_session_id`) that the event stands on; temporary, until events are written back to Google Calendar). A `community_events` row (an "event"; the table is not called `events`, which invites confusion with analytics events) holds only what the calendar and prickles have no place for (audience, registration, photos, focus...) and stands on exactly one prickle (`community_events.prickle_id`, NOT NULL, unique); its title, description, location, host, speakers and times are read from the `community_event_details` view (`EventDetail`, `lib/data/events.ts`), never stored on the event (see `docs/product/CALENDAR_EVENTS.md`).
