# Overview video: storyboard and script

Two narrated overview videos of Hedgie Hub for members, recorded from the demo community (`docs/DEMO_AND_HELP.md`, "Overview videos"). This is what is on screen and what the voice says; the words themselves live in `demo/video/script.ts` and are the captions too. Edit a line there and re-record (`pnpm demo:seed`, then `pnpm demo:video`); this file is the storyboard around them, so keep the two in step.

## Who it is for, and what it has to do

- **A new member** (or someone deciding whether to join) asks: *what is this, and why would I want it?* The answer is a community that makes writing less lonely, with a rhythm (Prickles, streaks) that keeps you going.
- **An existing member**, who already lives in Slack and Kajabi, asks: *what is in it for me now?* The answer is what the Hub adds to the habit they have: your schedule in your own calendar, your progress in one place, and the conversation (chat) without switching apps.
- **Voice**: warm, plain, a little proud; short sentences; one idea per beat. Everything said is something the screen shows. "Prickle" is explained the first time, never assumed.
- **Narrator**: a neutral, warm American female voice: Kokoro's `af_heart` (a local neural model; the setup and other voices are in `docs/DEMO_AND_HELP.md`). Captions are on the whole time, because most people will first see it with the sound off, on Instagram.
- **Cast**: women writers in a bookstagram voice (casual, emoji, word counts); the viewer is "Maya", three weeks in.

## Choices worth knowing about

- **Left out on purpose**: hosting (it opens after a member's first month, so it is not a first-week story), the Bookshelf and the Wheel of Wonder (nothing to show in the demo yet), and anything not live. The long cut shows chat because it is rolling out soon.
- **No prices, no sign-up call to action beyond "welcome to the Hedgie House"**: where it is shown (an email, the dashboard, Instagram) decides that, so it is a line to add per place.
- **The Google Calendar frames are stills from a real account**, because Google can't reach the demo's local feed; how they are made is in `docs/DEMO_AND_HELP.md`.
- **Pacing follows the voice**: each beat waits for whichever is longer, the sentence or the clicks, so a slower voice or a longer line lengthens the video by itself.
