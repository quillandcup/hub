# Product

How each feature works and why.

<!-- START docs-index generated: run `pnpm docs:index` to update, don't edit by hand -->

- [Usage analytics](ANALYTICS.md): How the Hub finds out whether a feature earns its place: a log of what members do, and funnels over it
- [Attendance Tracking Rules & Thresholds](ATTENDANCE_RULES.md): Attendance tracking uses time thresholds to split Zoom meetings into prickles, avoid double-counting early or late arrivals, and flag late hosts
- [Big Sister program](BIG_SISTERS.md): The Big Sister program matches each new member with an existing member for their first 30 days, to show them around and invite them to prickles
- [Events from the shared calendar](CALENDAR_EVENTS.md): Events are what members see in Events (retreats, workshops, speaker sessions), made from shared Google Calendar items or in the Hub, each standing on one prickle
- [Prickle Check-ins: What We Ask and Why](CHECKINS.md): A check-in is a few taps before and after a prickle, answered on the site or in Slack DMs
- [Check-ins and Check-outs: When and Where They Appear](CHECKIN_DELIVERY.md): Prickle check-in and check-out offers appear and disappear at set times, on set surfaces and for set members
- [Commitments](COMMITMENTS.md): A commitment is a member's promise to attend one or more recurring prickle slots for a set number of weeks
- [Courses in the Hub](COURSES.md): Quill & Cup's courses live in the Hub, synced from Kajabi until the Hub's course builder is good enough to replace it
- [Reproducible Demo and Help Docs](DEMO_AND_HELP.md): One mechanism, two outputs: a fictional demo community seeded into a local stack, and Playwright scenes that drive the app and capture screenshots and clips
- [The Finisher record](FINISHERS.md): The Finisher record holds a writer's milestones (wrote THE END, publish-ready, published), kept apart from the project they came from
- [Hedgie Care: tracking a community manager's reach-outs](HEDGIE_CARE.md): Hedgie Care tracks the community manager's reach-outs to members in the Hub, one care plan per member, in place of a spreadsheet
- [Landing pages (the page builder)](LANDING_PAGES.md): The page builder lets staff make and publish landing and website pages from blocks, on the Puck editor
- [Media library](MEDIA_LIBRARY.md): The media library is one place for the images the Hub shows, uploaded once, picked anywhere and resized as needed
- [Member Merge](MEMBER_MERGE.md): Merging combines two member records into one, transferring all associated data to the primary (kept) member and permanently deleting the secondary (duplicate)
- [Member Reminders](MEMBER_REMINDERS.md): The Hub nudges members on its own to introduce themselves on Slack and to put a prickle on their calendar, from one hourly job
- [Prospecting: finding ideal Hedgies on Instagram](PROSPECTING.md): Prospecting finds and ranks Instagram writers who would suit Quill & Cup, so daily outreach starts from a list
- [Slack-Bridged Chat: Design](SLACK_BRIDGED_CHAT.md): Hub chat mirrors our Slack channels, so a message typed in either place shows in both, and the Hub keeps the permanent archive
- [Writer profiles](WRITER_PROFILES.md): A member can opt in to a page of their own on the open web, at an address they choose: `/w/<handle>`
- [Writer titles](WRITER_TITLES.md): The words we use for members and their milestones, in the Hub, on public pages, in emails and in the changelog
